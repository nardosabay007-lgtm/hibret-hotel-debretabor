About Hibret Hotel

Discover our story, values, and commitment to Ethiopian hospitality in Debre Tabor

Welcome to Debre Tabor's Finest Stay

Situated in the historic heart of Debre Tabor, Hibret Hotel offers a refined blend of modern comfort and authentic Amhara warmth. Whether you are traveling for business, visiting regional attractions, or seeking a comfortable getaway, our doors are open to provide an unforgettably welcoming experience.

Our hotel features well-appointed accommodations, local and international dining options, versatile event spaces, and dedicated service staff available around the clock to meet every need.

Our Core Values

Genuine Hospitality

We treat every visitor like family, ensuring your stay feels comfortable and seamless from check-in to farewell.

Prime Location

Located centrally in Debre Tabor for easy accessibility to local business hubs, transportation, and regional landmarks.

Quality Accommodations

Clean, comfortable, and beautifully maintained rooms equipped with modern essentials for work and rest.

Cultural Connection

Celebrate Ethiopian culture through our authentic traditional dining options, fresh coffee ceremonies, and local expertise.
